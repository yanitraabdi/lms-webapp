INVERTA as a course book

A proposal for the whole site, public pages and learner app, in the same colours. This page is the proposal, not the build: nothing in the app has changed. Content shown is the real syllabus; the placeholder session descriptions are left out.

Visual thesis: INVERTA reads like a well-set course book: one reading column, the syllabus as its table of contents, chapters marked by type and white space instead of boxes. A box appears only around something you act on or keep: the price, a certificate, a result.

Allowed patterns: a dark hairline rule above each chapter heading; a running head (“Sesi 3 dari 8”); session numbers, because the program is strictly linear; tabular numerals for scores, times and prices.

  • Type does the work. Plus Jakarta Sans stays as the only face. Titles get much bigger (46px) and tighter; body text goes to 16px at a 60–65 character measure.
  • The page is the paper. Content sits on white, not as white cards on blue-grey. The blue-grey moves to quiet areas (code, notes, the app frame).
  • Removed everywhere: 👋 and other emoji, “→” arrow links, small purple uppercase eyebrows, blurred sticky headers, em dashes in our own copy, equal-card rows, numbered circles.
  • Kept: every colour token, Plus Jakarta Sans, the exam motion we just shipped, and the GR-14 wording on every score.

How the rest of the site follows

  • Auth pages: a plain title page on white, no floating card.
  • About, how it works, FAQ: reading column with chapter rules; FAQ as questions in bold with answers below, not boxed accordions.
  • Certificates, verify page, exam result: keep their box, since they are objects you keep.
  • Admin: same type scale and white pages; tables stay tables.
  • Copy pass: our own sentences lose em dashes and arrows. Session titles stored in the database are content and stay as entered.
